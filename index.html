Kin lafzon mein

Kin lafzon mein likhun yeh bhai-behen ka naata, Jo bina kahe samajh le, aisa koi aur nahi aata. Meri bekaar baaton ko bhi tu dil se sun leti hai, Main kuch na bhi kahun, tu sab kuch chun leti hai.

Dil se chuni behen

Na khoon ka rishta, na kisi kasam ki zaroorat, Kuch log bas mil jaate hain, rab ki inaayat. Tujhe behen maine dil se chuna hai, Is faisle pe bhai ko sabse zyada naaz hua hai.

Rakhi ka dhaaga

Kalaai pe bandha ek reshmi sa dhaaga, Uske saath bandh gaya bhai ka har ek waada. Tu maange na maange, main phir bhi nibhaunga, Teri ek muskaan ke liye duniya se lad jaaunga.

Bas ek call

Raat ho ya din, jab bhi dil ghabraaye, Kuch mat sochna behna, bas ek call aa jaaye. Galti teri ho ya saari duniya ki ho, Tera bhai tere saath khada milega, chahe jo bhi ho.

Kab subah ho gayi

Baatein shuru hoti hain “aaj kya hua” se, Aur pahunch jaati hain kisi bhoole hue qisse pe. Ghadi ki suiyaan chalti rehti hain chupke se, Aur subah ho jaati hai, hum dono ke hanste hanste.

Mood swings

Kabhi gussa, kabhi chup, kabhi pagal sa main, Har mood mein tune apnaaya, jaisa bhi hoon main. Tune kabhi badalne ko nahi kaha mujhe, Bas pyaar se samjhaaya, aur sambhaal liya mujhe.

Bigda sa bhai

Duniya ke liye main thoda sa pagal hoon, Tere liye wahi purana, bigda sa bachcha hoon. Tu daante bhi to lagta hai pyaar jaisa, Behna, koi nahi hai is duniya mein tere jaisa.

Nazar na lage 🧿

Teri hansi pe kisi ki nazar na lage, Tere sapnon pe kabhi koi asar na lage. Itni kaabil ban, itni buland ho jaaye, Ki tujhe kisi sahare ki zaroorat hi na aaye.

Teesra koi nahi

Galatfehmi aaye to baith ke suljha lena, Kisi teesre ki baaton pe dil mat laga lena. Yeh dosti bade pyaar se banaayi hai humne, Ise duniya ki nazar se bacha ke rakhna, behna.

Vishwas

Har kisi pe yakeen main aasaani se nahi karta, Har kisi ke aage dil ki kitaab nahi dharta. Par tujh pe, behna, pura vishwas hai mera, Tu saath ho to har andhera lagta hai savera.

Asli Diwali

Diwali se pehle hi duniya mein roshni aayi, Jis din tu aayi, rab ne asli Diwali manaayi. Teri har subah haseen, har shaam roshan rahe, Aur teri muskaan hamesha yun hi salaamat rahe.
